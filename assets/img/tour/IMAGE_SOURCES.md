# Hospitality image series

Created with the built-in ImageGen tool on 2026-11-11. These are illustrative concepts, not photographs of client properties. Original generated PNGs remain in the Codex generated_images directory. Website assets preserve the native 1672 × 941 resolution; smaller WebP and JPEG copies are delivery variants.

## Files

- `resort-coast.webp`: Aegean coastal resort, limestone pavilion, olive trees and infinity pool at blue hour.
- `boutique-courtyard.webp`: Cappadocian stone boutique-hotel courtyard with arches, linen seating and a quiet morning atmosphere.
- `bosphorus-hotel.webp`: contemporary waterfront city hotel, warm glazing, planted terrace and a distant Bosphorus bridge.
- `hotel-development.webp`: low-rise Mediterranean hotel under construction, realistic concrete structure, stone facade, scaffolding and a tower crane.

Each image also has a `-960.webp` and `-1280.jpg` variant in this directory.

## Prompt set

Shared direction: photorealistic editorial architectural imagery for a premium Turkish hospitality consultancy slider; one landscape 16:9 photograph per scene; natural materials, muted sea-green shadows and restrained warm highlights; meaningful architecture in the central/right part of the frame, quiet lower-left space for website copy and a strong subject for portrait crops. Realistic geometry, straight verticals, believable reflections. No text, logos, watermarks, borders, oversaturation or CGI appearance.

1. Aegean coastal resort: contemporary travertine pavilion, warm illuminated windows, mature olive trees and calm infinity pool meeting a deep teal sea, late blue hour, distant islands. The hotel and pool are the subject. No crowds or beach umbrellas.
2. Cappadocian boutique hotel: restored honey-colored stone arches, intimate courtyard, linen lounge seating, an olive tree, clay vessels and an open cave-suite doorway in soft morning light. Understated regional rock formations and two small distant balloons. The courtyard is the subject, without fantastical structures or excessive tourist props.
3. Istanbul city hotel: contemporary glass-and-stone waterfront facade and terrace at blue hour, warm window light, restrained furniture, planted grasses, dark turquoise water and a single distant Bosphorus suspension bridge. No landmark collage or generic invented skyline. Hotel architecture and terrace are the subject.
4. Mediterranean hotel development: active low-rise construction site with regular concrete columns and slabs, partially installed natural-stone facades, organized scaffolding, one accurately engineered tower crane and stacked building materials; teal bay and pine-clad headland in late-afternoon light. Clearly unfinished, without operating guest facilities or impossible crane geometry.
